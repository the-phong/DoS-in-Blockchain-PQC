**Disposition: PARTIAL; checklist box remains open.** Saturation was not observed within the approved safe range, so there is no measured saturation point at which to identify a natural bottleneck. The formal [R15 audit](../R15_BENIGN_CAPACITY_AUDIT.md) covers 36 fresh single-host RPC-origin benign networks at 20/23/25 valid tx/s across C/CP/P/H, three repetitions per cell. All 36 original run QC statuses are PASS. At 23 and 25 tx/s, zero of three runs per profile meet either registered knee branch; all four decisions are `NOT_OBSERVED_WITHIN_SAFE_RANGE`. Aligned timely fraction is 1.00 in every rate/profile cell. Eventual goodput equals offered rate in those short runs, but this is not sustainable steady-state throughput and cannot establish an absent knee beyond 25 tx/s.
