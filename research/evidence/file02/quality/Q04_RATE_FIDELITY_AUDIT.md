The current audit covered **248/248 fresh networks and 2,976/2,976 one-second steady windows**, including A13 off/on/off and the four order-balanced Q02 profiles. For every window it compared planned and measured monotonic **socket-write** counts and encoded bytes separately for total, fixed legitimate and background traffic. All achieved tx/s and byte/s matched the locked targets exactly at one-second resolution: zero windows beyond ±5%, zero below 90%, zero excluded runs. Maximum absolute per-event send-offset jitter was 4.038 ms across the audited set; per-run values and target/achieved windows are in the V2 JSON. A target of zero background tx/s in A13 off windows remained zero.

Offered rate is not confirmed valid throughput. For example, an A01 attack arm offered 241 distinct socket writes, while only 73 valid transactions committed; the other 168 were expected structural rejects. The audit uses `actual_offset_ns` and `wire_bytes` from successful generator socket writes, not accepted mempool count, RPC success count or eventual goodput. The existing RQ1b matched-service inferences need no rate-QC exclusions from this audit. One-second equality does not prove packet-level timing equality, sustainable capacity or network-origin fairness outside the tested loopback RPC topology.
